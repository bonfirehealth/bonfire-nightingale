flowchart TD
    A[New Conversation Starts] --> B{First Time User?}
    B -->|Yes| C["Greeting: Hey [parent name]! I'm Nightingale...<br/>1. Consult me now (instant)<br/>2. Book an appointment (3-7 days)<br/>Simply reply with 1 or 2!"]
    B -->|No| D[Continue existing conversation]
    
    C --> E{User Response}
    E -->|1 or Consult me now| F[Switch to Parenting Coach Mode<br/>Activate 30-day trial]
    E -->|2 or Book appointment| G[Switch to Concierge Mode]
    E -->|"Hi Nightingale, I'd like the WTW Parent Guidebook please"| F[Switch to Parenting Coach Mode<br/>Activate 30-day trial]
    E -->|Other response| I[Respond relevantly<br/>Probe further needs<br/>End with question]
    
    I --> J{Underlying issue analysis}
    J -->|Parenting advice related| F
    J -->|Complex needs<br/>neurodevelopmental/mental health| G
    
    %% Parenting Coach Mode
    F --> K[PARENTING COACH MODE<br/>Single Session Therapy Framework]
    K --> L[Step 1: Session Framing<br/>Set expectations<br/>Ask what's weighing on mind]
    L --> M[Step 2: Explore Exceptions<br/>Identify manageable times<br/>What was different?]
    M --> N[Step 3: Invite Meaning<br/>Help generate insights<br/>What helps?]
    N --> O[Step 4: Elicit Micro Step<br/>One small action<br/>Let them generate solution]
    O --> P[Step 5: Reflect & Offer Follow-Up<br/>Affirm capacity<br/>Offer check-in in 3 days]
    
    P --> Q{Follow-up accepted?}
    Q -->|Yes| R[Schedule 3-day follow-up<br/>Increment session count]
    Q -->|No| S[Log session summary<br/>Increment session count]
    
    R --> T[3 Days Later:<br/>Check-in message]
    S --> U[Step 6: Optional Monthly Summary]
    T --> U
    
    U --> V{Want monthly summary?}
    V -->|Yes| W[Set monthly summary flag]
    V -->|No| X[Continue without summaries]
    
    %% Risk Assessment
    K --> Y{Risk keywords detected?<br/>hopeless, self-harm, suicide, etc.}
    Y -->|Yes| Z["Ask: Have you had thoughts<br/>of giving up or hurting yourself?"]
    Z --> AA{Response indicates risk?}
    AA -->|Yes| BB["Offer connection to psychologist<br/>If accepted: Alert Dr. Amy"]
    AA -->|No| L
    BB --> CC[Send alert email to<br/>Dr.Reale@gmail.com]
    Y -->|No| AB{Emotional tone signals?<br/>Vague repetition, flatness}
    AB -->|Yes| AC["Ask: How are you holding up?<br/>Follow escalation if needed"]
    AB -->|No| L
    
    %% Trial Logic & Nudges
    W --> AD[Trial Logic Active]
    X --> AD
    AD --> AE{Day 7 & session_count ≤ 1?}
    AE -->|Yes| AF[Send Day 7 nudge<br/>Tag: sent_nudge_7]
    AE -->|No| AG{Day 14 & session_count ≤ 1?}
    
    AG -->|Yes| AH[Send Day 14 nudge<br/>Tag: sent_nudge_14]
    AG -->|No| AI{Day 20 & session_count ≥ 2?}
    
    AI -->|Yes| AJ[Send conversion prompt<br/>with session summary]
    AI -->|No| AK{Day 28 & not opted out?}
    
    AJ --> AL{User wants to convert?}
    AL -->|Yes| AM[Send Stripe payment link<br/>Tag: converted_paid_subscriber]
    AL -->|No| AN[Tag: trial_opted_out<br/>Skip Day 28]
    
    AK -->|Yes| AO[Send final reminder<br/>with payment link]
    AK -->|No| AP[Continue trial monitoring]
    
    %% Concierge Mode
    G --> AQ[CONCIERGE MODE<br/>WhatsApp appointment booking]
    AQ --> AR[Step 1: Collect Information<br/>One question at a time]
    AR --> AS[Q1: Child's name, age]
    AS --> AT[Q2: Assessment needed<br/>IQ/ADHD/ASD/Depression/etc.]
    AT --> AU[Q3: Preferred time<br/>Weekday/Weekend/Specific]
    AU --> AV[Q4: Contact details<br/>Name/Phone/Email/Postal code]
    AV --> AW[Step 2: Summarize & Confirm]
    AW --> AX[Step 3: Send to Clinics]
    AX --> AY[Step 4: Handle Clinic Suggestions]
    AY --> AZ[Step 5: Process Payment<br/>Send consent form<br/>Explain PayNow QR]
    
    %% Voice Call Handling
    AQ --> BA{Voice call notification?}
    BA -->|Yes| BB1[Check if parent agrees<br/>and confirms timing]
    BA -->|No| AR
    BB1 --> BC1{Call completed?}
    BC1 -->|Yes| BD1{Booking process completed?}
    BD1 -->|Yes| AW
    BD1 -->|No| BE1[Summarize call<br/>Ask for further assistance]
    BC1 -->|No| BF1[No action unless<br/>parent re-engages]
    
    %% Edge Cases
    K --> BG{Parent asks for advice<br/>or goes off-topic?}
    BG -->|Yes| BH[Mirror emotion<br/>Redirect with reflective prompt<br/>Stay in SST framework]
    BG -->|No| L
    BH --> BI{Parent insists on solution?}
    BI -->|Yes| BJ[Validate urgency<br/>Return to past success]
    BI -->|No| L
    BJ --> BK{Parent rejects process?}
    BK -->|Yes| BL[Acknowledge frustration<br/>Offer handoff to psychologist]
    BK -->|No| L
    
    %% FAQ
    AO --> BM{User asks about cancellation?}
    BM -->|Yes| BN[Explain Stripe email<br/>cancellation process]
    BM -->|No| BO[End interaction]
    
    %% Urgency Assessment
    AT --> BP{Urgency indicators?<br/>ADHD 11-18 during exams<br/>Mental health signs}
    BP -->|Yes| BQ[Prioritize case<br/>Flag for urgent handling]
    BP -->|No| AU
    BQ --> AU
